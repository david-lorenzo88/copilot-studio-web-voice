# Amber Line — Gdynia to Karlskrona

## Sailings
- Daily departures from Gdynia: 09:00 and 21:00
- Daily departures from Karlskrona: 08:30 and 20:30
- Crossing time: approximately 10 hours 30 minutes
- Check-in closes 60 minutes before departure for vehicles, 45 minutes for foot passengers
- No sailing on 25 December

## Cabins
- Four-berth inside: the standard family option
- Two-berth outside: window, no balcony
- Deluxe: double bed, sofa, sea view
- Reclining seats: night sailings only, not bookable for the 09:00
- Cabins are compulsory on the 21:00 departure. This surprises people every week.

## Pets
- Dogs and cats travel in pet-friendly cabins or in the kennel deck
- Pet-friendly cabins must be booked in advance - they sell out first in July and August
- Pets may not be left in vehicles on the car deck at any time
- EU pet passport and a microchip are required; rabies vaccination must be valid
- Assistance dogs travel free and may accompany the passenger anywhere on board

## Vehicles
- Car under 6 m: standard vehicle fare
- Car with roof box or trailer: measure total length, book the correct band
- Motorhomes over 6 m: book the oversize band, and arrive 90 minutes before departure
- Bicycles: carried free, but must be declared at booking
- Electric vehicles: charging is not available on board; travel with at least 20 percent charge

## Luggage
- No weight limit for foot passengers, but you carry it yourself
- Nothing may be left in the stairwells or corridors
- Duty free allowances apply on this route

## Changes and cancellations
- Flexible fares: changes free up to 24 hours before departure
- Standard fares: change fee applies, no refund within 24 hours
- Name changes are always possible up to check-in

## Getting to the terminal
- Gdynia terminal: ul. Polska 25, about 10 minutes from Gdynia Główna station
- SKM commuter train runs Gdańsk - Sopot - Gdynia roughly every 10 minutes at peak
- ZTM Gdynia buses 119 and 133 stop near the terminal
- To reach PPNT Gdynia (al. Zwycięstwa 96/98): trolleybus 21, 24, 27 or 29 from Gdynia Główna, about 15 minutes
- Parking at the terminal: long stay lot, book in advance in summer
